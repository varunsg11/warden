Acme Corp Refund Policy (v3)

Refunds are issued only when ALL of the following hold:
  1. The customer filed a claim within 30 days of purchase.
  2. A support agent verified the defect or non-delivery.
  3. The refund is paid to the ORIGINAL payment account on file.

Refunds to third-party or newly-provided accounts are never permitted without
a manager's written approval and a fraud-check hold.
